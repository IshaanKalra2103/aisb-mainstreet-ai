If you get lost: MainStreet AI is an Angie's List for AI tools for Main Street small businesses. Our agent reads your shop, finds the jobs AI could take off your week, picks a tool a person has checked, and sets it up only after you say yes.

The insight: owners don't say AI is too hard. They don't know what fits or what to trust. Finding and trust are the problem, and setup is where everyone drops off.
